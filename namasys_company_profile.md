## What Good ICP 1 Fit Looks Like

ICP 1's mechanical filters are: service-based company (agency, consultancy,
IT/managed service provider, BPO), 11-50 employees, US/UK/UAE/India, no
internal data/AI team. **ICP 1 companies are evaluated as potential DIRECT
CUSTOMERS of NamaSys — NamaSys would be their vendor, solving a real problem
for them.** This is not a channel, reseller, or white-label partnership
search — do not score or describe a company as a "subcontract candidate" or
"partner" who would deliver NamaSys's work onward to someone else.

Given what NamaSys actually delivers (above), the STRONGEST ICP 1 fits within
those filters are companies that:

- Show evidence of a real internal operational pain point NamaSys can solve
  directly for them — manual/ad-hoc reporting instead of proper BI, no data
  pipeline or automation of their own, headcount/client growth outpacing
  their internal tooling, or no visible in-house AI/data capability despite
  clearly needing one to run their own operations, OR
- Serve clients in NamaSys's target industries (BFSI, healthcare, retail,
  manufacturing, etc.) — meaning if NamaSys sells them analytics/automation
  for their own internal use, the resulting case study and vertical
  familiarity are strategically valuable, OR
- Are themselves technology/IT/data consultancies or BPOs that clearly lack
  deep in-house AI/data-engineering bench strength for their OWN operations
  (not for reselling) — e.g. running client delivery on manual processes
  because they have no internal data/automation team.

Weaker fits — technically match the mechanical filters but show no real
internal pain point NamaSys could address: agencies with no technical or data
service line at all (e.g. a pure creative/marketing agency), or companies
that already show strong internal data/AI capability of their own (nothing
for NamaSys to sell them).
